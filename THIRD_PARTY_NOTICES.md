# Third-party artwork

The emoji illustrations embedded in public/gift-art.js are from Twemoji, maintained by the jdecked/twemoji project: https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji

Graphics copyright Twitter, Inc. and other contributors, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The SVG artwork is embedded locally, scaled and placed into original story-card layouts. No external image request is needed at runtime. Exported story cards include a credit line. Keep this notice when redistributing the game.
